About ORSPEX

ORSPEX is an independent, bootstrapped software studio (Indie App Dev) dedicated to building high-performance, offline-first tools. We create premium desktop applications for professionals who value privacy, speed, and total control over their data.

We are a product-led studio, not a venture-backed machine. This means we answer directly to our users, not to advertisers or investors. We believe in sustainable entrepreneurship: building software that generates value through honest, one-time purchases, rather than draining your wallet with endless monthly subscriptions. We evolve by listening, shipping fast, and solving real problems for real professionals.

In an era where everything is moving to the cloud, we are building the opposite. No telemetry, no forced internet connections, and no data mining. Just powerful, standalone tools that work exactly when you need them—whether you're in a boardroom, an oil rig, or a basement with zero Wi-Fi.
