About ORSPEX

ORSPEX was founded on a simple principle: your software should work for you, not the other way around. We believe that your data belongs on your machine, not in a cloud server owned by a corporation.

In an era where everything is moving to the cloud, we are building the opposite. We create high-performance, standalone desktop applications that run entirely offline. No subscriptions, no telemetry, no forced internet connections. Just powerful tools that work when you need them.

🔒 Privacy First

Your data never leaves your computer. No tracking, no analytics, no cloud storage. Absolute privacy by design.

⚡ High Performance

By utilizing your local hardware, our tools run significantly faster than web-based alternatives.

💼 Built for Pros

Whether you are a forensic investigator, a business owner, or a video editor, our suite has you covered.

♾️ Own It Forever

No monthly fees. Buy a license once and use the software for as long as you need it.
