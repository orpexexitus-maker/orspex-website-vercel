Privacy Statement

Effective · 1 January 2026 Last updated · 1 January 2026 Version · 1.0

The short version

ORSPEX Core is a stand-alone, offline-first application. It does not connect to the internet, does not require an account, and does not transmit your data anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your device — in a local encrypted file that you own and control.

01 Overview

This Privacy Statement explains how ORSPEX ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the ORSPEX software suite, including ORSPEX Core, and our website.

We built ORSPEX on a single principle: your data belongs to you. ORSPEX Core is a stand-alone desktop application. It runs entirely on your own computer, and it does not send any data to us or to any third party.

02 Information we do not collect

To be direct: we do not collect any personal information from users of ORSPEX Core. The application does not have the ability to send data anywhere — because it has no network access and no cloud component.

We do not collect:
  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Your company, customers, suppliers, or any business records
  • Invoices, journal entries, inventory, or financial data
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Cookies, advertising IDs, or tracking pixels

There is no account, no sign-up, no newsletter subscription, and no telemetry switch to disable — because none of these exist in the application.

03 Information you create locally

Everything you enter into ORSPEX Core — company information, customers, suppliers, products, invoices, journal entries, tax codes, reports, and any other business record — is stored locally on your computer.

This data is yours. It is not transmitted to us, and we never see it. It is stored in a single local file that you can back up, move, or delete at any time.

If you uninstall ORSPEX Core, your data file remains on your device until you delete it yourself.

04 Local security

Your data file is encrypted at rest. Access is controlled by your Windows user account and any password or access controls you set on your machine.

Because ORSPEX Core is single-user and offline, it has a much smaller attack surface than cloud ERPs. There are no cloud credentials to leak, no shared multi-tenant database, and no remote endpoint to breach.

The most important security measure is the security of your own device: keep Windows updated, use a strong account password, and keep your data file in a safe location.

05 Backups & portability

You are in full control of your data. To back up your entire business, simply copy your data file to another location — a USB drive, an external disk, or your own private backup storage.

We do not operate any backup service, and we do not have access to any copy of your data. Backups are your responsibility, and we recommend making them regularly.

If you choose to store your data file in a cloud-synced folder (such as OneDrive, Dropbox, or Google Drive), please be aware that this is your decision and that the privacy practices of that provider apply. We do not require or recommend this.

06 This website

This website is designed to be as simple as the application. We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third-party cookies.

When you visit this site, your browser may store your language preference locally (using standard browser storage) so that the site remembers your choice on your next visit. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

If you contact us by email, we will use your email address only to reply to your message. We do not add you to mailing lists and we do not share your address with anyone.

07 Your rights

Because we do not collect your personal data, most data-subject rights — such as access, correction, deletion, or portability — are already satisfied by design: you have full and exclusive access to your own data at all times.

If you believe we have somehow received information about you and wish to exercise a legal right under a privacy law such as the GDPR, the UAE PDPL, or the PIPL, you may contact us using the details below, and we will respond promptly.

08 Changes to this statement

If we ever change this Privacy Statement, we will update the version number and the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on our website.

Our core commitment will not change: ORSPEX Core is offline-first, and your data stays on your device.

09 Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Statement or about how ORSPEX handles data, please reach out.

  • Email: privacy@orspex.com
